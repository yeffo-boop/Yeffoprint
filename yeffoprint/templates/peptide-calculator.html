The math

How it's calculated

Three steps, shown with your numbers. A U-100 syringe holds 100 units per mL, so every unit is 0.01 mL.

  1. Concentration
    peptide (mg) ÷ water (mL)5 mg ÷ 2 mL = 2.5 mg/mL
  2. Volume per dose
    dose ÷ concentration0.25 mg ÷ 2.5 mg/mL = 0.1 mL
  3. Units to draw
    volume (mL) × 1000.1 mL × 100 = 10 units
